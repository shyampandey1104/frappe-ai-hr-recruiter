# 🧑‍💼 Frappe AI HR Recruiter & Candidate Screening Agent

[![Python 3.9+](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.9+-blue.svg)](https://www.python.org/downloads/)
[![Frappe HRMS](https://img.shields.io/badge/Frappe-HRMS-blue.svg)](https://frappehr.com)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An intelligent **Resume Screening & Job Fit Evaluation Agent** for **Frappe HRMS**. Parses applicant resumes, extracts technical skills and professional tenure, evaluates semantic keyword overlap against open Job Openings, and assigns automated fit scorecards (`Shortlist`, `Review`, `Reject`).

---

## ⚡ Highlights
- 📄 **Resume NLP Parsing**: Extracts skills, certifications, and years of experience.
- 🎯 **Job Fit Scoring**: 0-100% weighted evaluation against mandatory & preferred skills.
- 🔄 **Frappe HRMS Integration**: Syncs match scores directly to `Job Applicant` DocTypes.
- 🧪 **Pytest Coverage**: Unit tests included.
